Stranger Things is a science fiction horror series created by *The Duffer Brothers*.  
It’s set in the 1980s and follows a group of kids who encounter supernatural events in their small town of Hawkins, Indiana.

---

- Friendship and loyalty  
- Government secrets and experiments  
- The supernatural and alternate dimensions (the *Upside Down*)  


1. **Eleven** — a girl with telekinetic powers.  
2. **Mike Wheeler** — brave and loyal friend.  
3. **Jim Hopper** — the town’s police chief.  
4. **Joyce Byers** — determined mother searching for her son.  

---

> “Friends don’t lie.” – *Eleven*

---

I love **Stranger Things** because of its mix of **nostalgic 80s style**, **suspenseful storytelling**, and **strong character bonds**.  
Every season feels like a new adventure full of mystery and emotion.
